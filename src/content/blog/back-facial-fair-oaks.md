A back facial gives your back the same steps your face gets in a facial: a deep cleanse, exfoliation, extractions and a mask. Mine is called the Back Reset. It's 60 minutes and $160.

## Why would I need a back facial?

Your back is hard to reach and quick to break out. You can't see it well, and you can't clean it the way you clean your face.

The [Back Reset](/services/back-reset/) is best for back breakouts, clogged pores and rough texture.

## What happens during the Back Reset?

Here's the order.

1. A deep cleanse
2. An enzyme or light peel
3. Steam, to soften the skin
4. Extractions
5. High frequency or LED light
6. A purifying mask
7. Hydration

The peel and steam come first so extractions are easier on your skin. The light and the mask come after, to help calm things down.

## Does it hurt?

Steam softens your skin before extractions, and a mask calms it afterward. Mild redness can last a few hours.

## What should I wear?

A loose top.

## What should I do afterward?

Skip heavy workouts and hot showers for the rest of the day. That's the main thing.

## When should I book before an event?

For a wedding or a vacation, book it one to two weeks ahead. That leaves your skin time to settle.

## I break out on my face too. Can I do both?

Yes, as two bookings. The [Clear Skin Facial](/services/clear-skin-facial/) is my facial for breakouts and congested skin. It's 75 minutes and $160.

Breakout-prone skin responds best to a series, so ask me how to space them.

## Is this a medical treatment?

No. It's cosmetic skincare. Severe or painful acne is best seen by a dermatologist first. And tell me if you use prescription acne medication.
