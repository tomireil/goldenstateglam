Before a facial, the main job is telling me what's going on with your skin. After, it's leaving your skin alone. Here's the detail for each kind of visit.

## Before any facial

- **Come with or without makeup.** The double cleanse takes care of it.
- **Tell me when you book** about any allergies, prescriptions, or recent skin treatments.
- **Mention acne medication.** If you use a prescription for acne, I need to know.

## Before a deep cleansing facial

Skip retinol and exfoliating acids for three days before a [Signature Deep Cleanse](/services/signature-deep-cleanse/). More on that in [Using Retinol? What to Know Before a Facial](/blog/retinol-before-facial-fair-oaks/).

## Before a men's facial

Shave the day before, not the morning of your visit. Beards are welcome at the [Men's Deep Clean Facial](/services/mens-deep-clean/).

## Before a back facial

Wear a loose top to the [Back Reset](/services/back-reset/).

## Before a peel

Stop retinol and exfoliating acids five days before a [Chemical Peel](/services/chemical-peel/).

Peels aren't suitable during pregnancy, on sunburned skin, or within six months of taking isotretinoin.

## What's normal after a facial?

- **After extractions:** slight redness. It usually fades within a few hours.
- **After the Clear Skin Facial:** some redness that settles within a day.
- **After a hydrating facial:** nothing to manage. Nothing harsh is used in the [Korean Glass Skin Facial](/services/korean-glass-skin/), so redness is unlikely.

## What should I do after a back facial?

Skip heavy workouts and hot showers for the rest of the day.

## What should I do after a gua sha or massage facial?

Drink plenty of water after the [Sculpt & Drain Facial](/services/sculpt-drain-facial/).

## What should I do after a peel?

This is the one with real rules.

- Stay out of the sun
- Skip heavy workouts
- Don't pick at flaking skin for a week
- Wear sunscreen every day

Expect mild flaking for two to five days after a Chemical Peel. I give you aftercare instructions before you leave.

## What will I take home?

After a [New Client Signature Facial](/services/new-client-signature/), you leave with a simple home care plan. After a Clear Skin Facial, a simple routine for home.
