If you have not booked at Golden State Glam before, start with the [New Client Signature Facial](/services/new-client-signature/). It is $150 and 80 minutes. We begin with a short consultation and a close look at your skin, then a full facial built around what your skin needs that day. The studio is at 7996 California Ave, Suite A, Fair Oaks. People also drive in from Carmichael, Sacramento, and nearby neighborhoods. There is only one location.

The menu is a set of cosmetic facials. It is not a medical visit, and it does not promise a particular result. Read the price, the length, and what is included, then book the one that fits.

## Start with one goal

**Cleansing.** The [Signature Deep Cleanse Facial](/services/signature-deep-cleanse/) is $180 and 90 minutes. Choose it when you already want a thorough cleanse. It includes cleansing, exfoliation, extractions when appropriate, hydration, and finishing SPF. New clients have a $50 deposit on the published menu. The [Men’s Deep Clean Facial](/services/mens-deep-clean/) is $180 and 90 minutes, with attention to skin that is sensitive from shaving. The [Clear Skin Facial](/services/clear-skin-facial/) is $160 and 75 minutes, for breakouts and congestion.

**Hydration.** The [Korean Glass Skin Facial](/services/korean-glass-skin/) is $165 and 75 minutes, with serum, a hydrating mask, and facial massage.

**Relaxation.** [Facial massage and relaxation](/services/#glow) is the slower end of the menu. The Lymphatic Drainage Facial Massage is $110 and 70 minutes, with a $50 deposit. The Deep Relax & Sleep Ritual is $180 and 90 minutes, with an $80 deposit for new clients. Facial Sculpting Therapy is $150 and 80 minutes, a moisture-first facial for dry or mature skin with facial massage. The Deep Relax & Sleep Ritual is self-care, with massage, warm towels, and aromatherapy. It is not a treatment for anxiety or sleep disorders.

## Match the time you actually have

A facial that looks right on paper is the wrong booking if you need to leave early. The New Client Signature Facial is 80 minutes. Korean Glass Skin is 75 minutes. Signature Deep Cleanse is 90 minutes. The Clear Skin Facial is 75 minutes. The Deep Relax ritual is 90 minutes. Give yourself the full block, plus a few minutes to arrive and settle.

The studio is open Monday, Wednesday, Friday, and Saturday, 10 AM–6 PM. Tuesday, Thursday, and Sunday are closed. An open day does not mean every hour is free. Check the booking site for a time.

## Say what your skin has been through

Before the appointment, share sensitivities, allergies, recent procedures, and products that might change what should be used. Extractions, when a facial includes them, happen only if they are appropriate that day. If you are choosing for a teen, contact the studio about age and guardian requirements before you book.
