Say your work party is on a Friday in mid-December. You’ve never had a facial with me, and you’re eyeing the Wednesday before. You could. But a first visit pressed right up against an event leaves you no slack if your plans change. Here’s how I’d think it through.

I won’t hand you a magic number. It depends on the facial, your skin and whether you’ve had one with me before. What I can say: the earlier you mention the event, the more choices you have.

## Is the week before a party a good time to try new skincare?

Usually not. The [American Academy of Dermatology](https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/skin-care-secrets/prevent-skin-problems/test-skin-care-products) suggests testing a new product on a small spot twice a day for seven to 10 days before using it, and notes that ingredients like retinol and glycolic acid can irritate, especially sensitive skin. So if a new serum is part of your holiday plan, count backward from the party.

Already using retinol? Read [what to know before a facial](/blog/retinol-before-facial-fair-oaks/) first.

## What should I tell you before my appointment?

Your event date. Whether this is your first facial with me. The products you use now, especially anything new. Add sensitivities, allergies and recent procedures. My [FAQ](/faq/) asks for the same basics before any facial.

## Can a facial fix a breakout before my party?

I can’t promise that. My facials are cosmetic skincare, not medical treatment, and one visit doesn’t guarantee a particular look. If a breakout is painful, deep or won’t settle, see a board-certified dermatologist, ideally well before the event.
