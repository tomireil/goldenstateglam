## Why do the prices differ?

It comes down to what's in the visit and how long it runs. Peels are priced by the product, which is why the Chemical Peel has a range.

The price you see covers the whole appointment listed on that service's page.

## Is there a deposit?

For most services, yes. The booking menu lists a $50 deposit on most facials and $80 on some longer or more advanced treatments. In many cases it's for new clients only.

Whatever the booking screen shows is the one to follow.

## Do packages lower the price?

Yes, if you come regularly. Both packages are built on the Signature Deep Cleanse.

- Three sessions: $510. Regular value $540, so you save $30.
- Six sessions: $990. Regular value $1,080, so you save $90.

That works out to $170 a visit on the three pack and $165 on the six pack, against $180 for a single visit.

## Which one should I book if price matters?

Start with the New Client Signature Facial at $150. It includes a consultation, so you leave knowing which facial fits and how often to come. That saves you guessing with the next booking.
