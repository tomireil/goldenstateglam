## Are extractions always included?

No. The menu says “extractions when appropriate for your skin that day.” That wording is deliberate. Skin changes from visit to visit, so whether extractions make sense is part of the appointment, not something decided by the menu.

So if you book this facial only because you want extractions, know that they aren’t guaranteed. And if you were hoping to avoid them, you aren’t signing up for them automatically.

## What should I tell you about my skin?

The more specific, the better. Tell me about sensitivities, allergies, recent procedures and the skincare products you use. If you feel strongly about extractions either way, say that too. It belongs in the same conversation.

## Should I squeeze blackheads at home instead?

I’d skip it. The American Academy of Dermatology warns that popping pimples at home can push contents deeper into the skin, which can lead to more noticeable acne, pain, infection or scarring. For everyday washing, the AAD suggests a gentle, non-abrasive cleanser without alcohol, lukewarm water, your fingertips, and no scrubbing.

## Is a deep cleanse facial an acne treatment?

No. My facials are cosmetic skincare, not medical treatment, and a single visit doesn’t promise a particular result. If breakouts are painful, keep coming back, or don’t improve, see a board-certified dermatologist.
