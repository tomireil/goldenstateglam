Book a facial if you want clean, hydrated skin with no downtime. Book a chemical peel if you want a more even tone and you can plan for a few days of flaking.

That's the short version. Here's the longer one.

## What does a facial do?

A facial cleans, exfoliates gently, and puts moisture back. Depending on the one you choose, it can include extractions, a mask and facial massage.

You walk out with skin that looks good the same day. Slight redness after extractions is normal and usually fades within a few hours.

## What does a chemical peel do?

A peel uses skincare acids to loosen the dull top layer of skin so fresher skin shows through.

My [Chemical Peel](/services/chemical-peel/) is 45 minutes and costs $120 to $170, depending on the peel. I look at your skin first, then choose the one that suits it.

Expect light tingling during the peel. Then mild flaking for two to five days. After that, skin looks smoother and more even.

## How do they compare?

- **Goal.** A facial is for clean, calm, hydrated skin. A peel is for dullness, uneven tone, fine lines and marks left by breakouts.
- **Downtime.** A facial has none. A peel means two to five days of mild flaking.
- **Prep.** Before a deep cleansing facial, skip retinol and exfoliating acids for three days. Before a peel, stop them five days ahead.
- **After.** After a peel, stay out of the sun, skip heavy workouts, don't pick at flaking skin for a week, and wear sunscreen every day.

## When is a facial the better choice?

- It's your first visit
- You have an event this week
- Your skin feels dry, tight or congested
- You want to relax

## When is a peel the better choice?

- Your skin looks dull no matter what you put on it
- You have marks left over from old breakouts
- Your tone looks uneven
- You have a clear week ahead with no big plans

## Who shouldn't have a peel?

Peels aren't suitable during pregnancy, on sunburned skin, or within six months of taking isotretinoin. Tell me about prescriptions and recent treatments when you book.
