Visiting from Sacramento for your first facial with me? Start with the New Client Signature Facial. It’s where first-time clients begin at my Fair Oaks studio, and you leave knowing which facial fits and how often to come.

Say you’re in Midtown Sacramento, looking at my menu on your phone. The Korean Glass Skin Facial sounds nice. So does the Signature Deep Cleanse. You’d rather not make the trip out to Fair Oaks and pick the wrong one. Fair enough. Here’s how I’d think it through.

## Why not just pick a facial from the menu?

You can read the menu, and you should. It has hydration facials, a deep cleanse, a men’s facial, a facial for breakouts and massage-focused visits. Each page says who it’s for.

But a description can’t see your skin. On a first visit, you’re choosing from words, not from a look at your skin. That’s why first-time clients start with the New Client Signature Facial.

Want the wider view of how the menu is organized? Read [How to choose your first facial in Fair Oaks](/blog/choosing-your-first-facial/).

## What happens in the New Client Signature Facial?

Here it is in plain terms. We start with a short consultation and a look at your skin. Then you get a facial built around what your skin needs that day. There’s no downtime.

And you leave knowing which facial fits and how often to come. The [New Client Signature Facial](/services/new-client-signature/) page has the details.

## Why does that matter if you’re visiting from Sacramento?

Because the trip is part of your plan. Once you know which facial fits and how often to come, you can plan your next visits around your own schedule. You aren’t back on the menu, guessing again.

Every appointment happens at one studio: 7996 California Ave, Suite A, Fair Oaks. There’s no Sacramento location.

## What if I already know I want a deep cleanse?

Tell me when you book. The [Signature Deep Cleanse Facial](/services/signature-deep-cleanse/) is a regular service on my menu. It can include extractions, but only when they’re appropriate for your skin that day.

For a first visit, though, start with the New Client Signature Facial. If a deeper cleanse is what fits, you’ll leave knowing that.

## What should I tell you before my first facial?

When you book, tell me about allergies, prescriptions and recent skin treatments. If you use retinol, read [Using Retinol? What to Know Before a Facial](/blog/retinol-before-facial-fair-oaks/) first.

You don’t need to arrive knowing your skin type. The American Academy of Dermatology’s [everyday skin care advice](https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/skin-care-basics/care/skin-care-tips-dermatologists-use) is to use products that match your skin’s needs, whether it’s oily, dry, normal, combination or sensitive. Choosing a facial follows the same logic: start from your skin, not the menu.

## Is a facial a treatment for a skin condition?

No. My facials are cosmetic skincare, not medical treatment, and I can’t promise a particular result from one visit. If something is painful, spreading or not settling, see a board-certified dermatologist.
