Most of my clients come back every four to six weeks. That's the right pace for a regular facial. A few situations run on a different clock, so here they are.

## Every four to six weeks: regular facials

This is the rhythm for the [Signature Deep Cleanse](/services/signature-deep-cleanse/), my deep cleansing facial.

If that's you, look at the packages. Both are built on the Signature Deep Cleanse.

- Three sessions for $510. You save $30.
- Six sessions for $990. You save $90.

## Every two weeks at first: breakouts

Breakout-prone skin responds best to a series. With the [Clear Skin Facial](/services/clear-skin-facial/), most clients start with six visits, two weeks apart.

## Regular visits: firming and sculpting

The [Lift & Firm Facial](/services/lift-firm-facial/) makes skin look more awake the same day, and the effect builds with regular visits.

The same goes for the [Sculpt & Drain Facial](/services/sculpt-drain-facial/). The de-puffing is temporary, and results build when you keep coming.

## Whenever you need it: relaxation

The [Deep Relax & Sleep Ritual](/services/deep-relax-sleep-ritual/) is a relaxation ritual first and a skin treatment second. Book it when you need to switch off. There's no schedule to keep.

## Before an event

Timing depends on the treatment.

- Lift & Firm Facial: three to five days ahead
- [Back Reset](/services/back-reset/): one to two weeks ahead
- [Exopeel Premium Skin Renewal](/services/exopeel-premium-skin-renewal/): at least two weeks ahead

Peels flake. A [Chemical Peel](/services/chemical-peel/) means mild flaking for two to five days, so count back from your date.
