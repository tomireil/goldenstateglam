“Glow” is an aim, not a measurement. The hydration facial at Golden State Glam is the Korean Glass Skin Facial. It is given at 7996 California Ave, Suite A, Fair Oaks. Clients visit that studio from Fair Oaks, Folsom, El Dorado Hills, Carmichael, and Sacramento.

These are cosmetic visits. Skin responds individually. Nothing on the menu promises that a look will last, or that a facial is right for every person.

## Korean Glass Skin Facial

The [Korean Glass Skin Facial](/services/korean-glass-skin/) is $165 and 75 minutes.

The visit includes cleansing, exfoliation, serum application, a hydrating mask, and relaxing facial massage. It is the one to read if you want a dewy, refreshed appearance and you have 75 minutes. The name describes the style of the facial. It does not mean skin will look a specific way afterward.

## What to share ahead of time

The hydration facial still uses exfoliation, serum, and a mask. Tell me about sensitivities, allergies, recent procedures, and the products you already use. That is how the steps stay appropriate. Preparation advice should be specific to the facial you choose, so ask when you book.
